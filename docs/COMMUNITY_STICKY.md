## ⬇️ Download & Install

**Current build: Update 0.1.3 "The Row Is Open"** → Patch notes: [PATCH_NOTES_URL_0.1.3]

**Requirements:** Windows 10/11 (64-bit), ~200 MB disk space, internet not required — the game runs fully offline on your machine.

## 🎮 Your First 10 Minutes

1. **Create your citizen** — distribute your Power/Intelligence/Charisma/Luck and pick a special ability (you can re-roll)
2. **Read the Field Guide** — six short briefings covering the city loop; skippable and replayable anytime from the system panel
3. **Walk The Block** — you spawn at your home. NPCs wander the map in real time; walk up and press **E** to talk
4. **Watch the clock** — NPCs live on schedules. Ace leaves for downtown at noon, Tess hits the casino after 7 PM, club staff arrive at night. The sidebar's **Service Contacts** roster tells you who is where, right now
5. **Mind your Energy** — work, training, and the night scene all cost Energy. Sleep at your home to recover and start a new day

## ⚖️ Choose Your Path

Everything you do pushes you toward one of three identities:

- **Angel** — order, legitimacy, civic power
- **Ghost** — information, markets, quiet leverage
- **Demon** — power, fear, underworld respect

Your faction changes which districts open up, which shops and services you can access, which contracts you're offered, and how NPCs react to you. Faction quest arcs end with named outcomes (Coalition Steward, The Quiet Broker, The Enforcer…) that permanently reshape the city.

## ❓ FAQ

**Do my saves carry over between updates?** Yes. Old saves initialize safely with any new systems on first load.

**Is there a web or mobile version?** The official web-playable and mobile versions are the next major distribution step — the Windows build is our current testing channel.

**Is combat mandatory?** No. Every major fight can be resolved socially or economically if you've built the Charisma, Intelligence, or wealth for it.

**Why did an NPC just walk up to me?** Street encounters — roaming characters approach you with offers or warnings. Warnings matter: ignore Tommy's pickpocket alert in the Underground Markets and you will lose real cash.

**Something's broken / I found a bug.** Post it here with your build number (shown on the main menu, e.g. `build v0.1.3`) and what you were doing when it happened.

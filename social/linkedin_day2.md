# Day 2: The Visual System (Phases 5-8)

**Hook:**
"Quiet power. Not noisy cyberpunk."

**Body:**
We’re locking in the core visual identity for Phase 5-8. The goal isn't just "dark mode" — it's about creating a spiritual-street atmosphere that feels premium and restrained.

Here is the breakdown of the new system:

5️⃣ **HUD LAYOUT**
Minimalist. Top bar for context (Day, Location, Wealth), bottom bar for navigation. No clutter, no icons unless they fight for their life to be there.
*Style:* Inter ExtraBold headers, JetBrains Mono data.

6️⃣ **TRADING VIEW**
Directly inspired by high-frequency trading terminals, but cleaner.

- Line charts only (no bars).
- Soft glow strokes (Ghost Blue / Demon Red).
- "Live Feed" indicators that pulse with the network status.
- Glassmorphism panels (`.coalition-panel`) that feel like pressed glass over void.

7️⃣ **MAP VISUALS**
Moving away from generic city maps.

- **Ground:** Matte black / desk texture.
- **Buildings:** Paper cutout cards with slight shadows.
- **Districts:** Thin glowing outlines.
- It’s not a map; it’s a board.

8️⃣ **CHARACTER STYLES**
From MVP to Premium.

- Silhouette-based "Stick" aesthetic but upgraded.
- Alignment Glows:
  - 😇 **Angel:** Soft halo ring (#e8e8e8)
  - 👻 **Ghost:** Cyan outline (#6ddcff)
  - 👹 **Demon:** Red edge shadow (#ff3b3b)

**Why this works:**
Deep blacks. Soft glows. Strong typography.
If it adds noise and doesn't serve gameplay -> **cut it.**
